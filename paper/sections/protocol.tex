\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Pre-registration.} Rounds 4 and 5 and the \sysI/\sysII{} comparison were pre-registered: cells,
policies, primary metric, comparisons, thresholds and seeds were written down and committed to the repository
before any model policy was run on the corresponding test tasks \citep{nosek2018preregistration}. The round~6
pre-registration is drafted and will be frozen in its own commit before any test episode. Earlier rounds and
the sprint are exploratory and are labelled so. Analyses designed after seeing registered results are labelled
\emph{exploratory} and were run on fresh seeds. Errata to registrations are recorded, not silently fixed
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{Paired evaluation.} Within a cell (robot $\times$ family), every policy runs on the same seeded
tasks. We report paired mean differences with 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals over tasks
\citep{efron1979bootstrap,efron1993introduction}. A difference is called significant when the interval
excludes zero. There is no pooling across robots and no multiple-comparison correction; every registered cell is
reported, including nulls.

\paragraph{Sample size.} Closed-loop cells use $n = 30$ paired tasks (or lots) unless stated; cells that pool
three training seeds have $n = 90$, and the synthetic-world comparison has $n = 300$. With $n = 30$ the
intervals are wide (typically $\pm 0.1$ on a success rate), which limits what a single cell can show.

\paragraph{Tuning.} Every threshold is chosen on the validation split and then frozen: the probing threshold
$\tau = 0.15$, the competence gate (validation AUC $\ge 0.8$), and the shared budget in round~5, which was
selected by a rule fixed before the validation baselines were seen and using scripted baselines only.

\paragraph{Held-out conditions.} The Gen3 arm appears in no training set and no selection step. The
\fm{drawer_open} family is excluded from training and validation data from round~3.

\paragraph{Metrics.} Episode success (environment predicate on true state); for lots, \emph{good parts per lot},
the fraction of parts that end in the tote and undamaged, which was registered as primary because episode
success is near zero for all policies on arm lots; damage rate; probes per lot; effort; simulated time.
Timeouts and crashes inside an episode count as failures.

\paragraph{Baselines.} Scripted: nominal, \pta{}, force-ramp, and the oracle-parameter script (labelled).
Learned: the generic-memory and no-memory variants of the world model under the same planner; the oracle planner
(true latents in the slots; labelled); and, in round~6, ACT imitation \citep{zhao2023act} trained from scratch
on oracle-parameter demonstrations, with the memory models also trained in a \emph{budget-matched} regime on
exactly the same episodes. Both sides get a validation tuning grid of comparable effort.

\paragraph{Reporting rules.} Oracle rows use privileged labels and are always marked. Gantry results are kept
apart from arm results. Absolute success rates are reported alongside differences.
