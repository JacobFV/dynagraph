\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Online system identification and adaptation.}
Conditioning a policy on an online estimate of hidden dynamics parameters is a standard route to robustness
\citep{yu2017uposi,kumar2021rma}. Those estimates are usually an unstructured latent consumed by a policy
network. We instead keep one labelled slot per physical quantity, which makes the estimate inspectable and
allows interventions that test whether it is used.

\paragraph{Structured and set-valued memory.}
Our encoders use object-centric and set-based components: slot attention over entity tokens
\citep{locatello2020slot}, attention pooling and cross-attention between a small latent array and an unordered
set \citep{lee2019set,jaegle2021perceiver}, on top of the transformer \citep{vaswani2017attention}. Learned
physical reasoning over objects and relations goes back at least to interaction networks
\citep{battaglia2016interaction}. Feature-wise modulation \citep{perez2018film} is how the typed slot values
enter the outcome head.

\paragraph{Model-based planning under uncertainty.}
The planner optimises skill parameters with the cross-entropy method \citep{rubinstein1999cross} and uses
ensemble disagreement as epistemic uncertainty, following probabilistic-ensemble model-based control
\citep{chua2018pets,lakshminarayanan2017ensembles}.

\paragraph{Imitation learning baselines.}
Action chunking with transformers (ACT) \citep{zhao2023act}, a conditional VAE \citep{kingma2014vae} policy
that predicts action chunks and ensembles them over time, is our end-to-end imitation baseline.

\paragraph{Fast and slow pathways in one model.}
The \sysI/\sysII{} vocabulary comes from dual-process accounts of cognition \citep{kahneman2011thinking}.
Vision-language-action models such as RT-2 \citep{brohan2023rt2} put high-level reasoning and low-level action
in one pretrained network. Our variant uses a looped (weight-shared, recurrent-depth) transformer
\citep{dehghani2019universal} and low-rank adapters \citep{hu2022lora} that are switched on only for the fast
pathway, so that the slow pathway remains the unmodified pretrained model.

\paragraph{Evaluation practice.}
Small-sample comparisons in learning-based control are fragile \citep{henderson2018matters,agarwal2021precipice}.
We use paired designs with bootstrap intervals \citep{efron1979bootstrap,efron1993introduction} and
pre-registration \citep{nosek2018preregistration}.
