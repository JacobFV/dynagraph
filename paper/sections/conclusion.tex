\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An explicit typed memory of hidden physics is easy to motivate and, in our experience, hard to make matter. A
first design learned to ignore its own friction slot. A second design, in which history can reach the
prediction head only through the typed slots and fragility is observable through an actor-legal tactile cue,
sharpened a lot's crush estimate after one probe in a diagnostic case and carried it to later parts. In closed loop this
consistently beats a generic-memory model and reduces probing, but it turns into more good parts than a simple
probe-then-act script only where the embodiment is competent enough for saved calls to matter, which so far is
the gantry under a shared budget ($+0.09$ \ci{+0.03}{+0.16}, pre-registered). On the arms the limiting factor is
the skill layer.

The next results, both pre-registered, will show whether the approach holds against end-to-end imitation on
repaired skills and whether a pretrained looped language model can host the fast pathway. We will report them
whichever way they come out.
